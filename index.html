THE AMERICAN ECONOMIST

The three pots.

Give your money a purpose by dividing it between three simple pots.

01 ESSENTIALS

Needs

Money for the things you need to live, such as food, bills, rent, transport and other essential expenses.

02 LIFESTYLE

Wants

Money for things you enjoy but don't necessarily need, such as eating out, entertainment, shopping and hobbies.

03 FUTURE

Savings

Money you put aside for your goals, emergencies and future plans. This is where SaveTarget comes in.

SAVING TIPS

Small changes, big savings.

Simple ways to make saving money easier and stay on track with your goal.

01

Set a weekly limit

Give yourself a realistic spending limit for the week and try to stay within it.

02

Wait before buying

Give yourself 24 or 48 hours before making an unnecessary purchase. You may find you don't want it anymore.

03

Cook at home

Preparing meals at home can help reduce the amount you spend on takeaway food and restaurants.

04

Cancel unused subscriptions

Check your subscriptions and remove anything you no longer use.

05

Compare prices

Before buying something, compare different shops and look for a cheaper alternative.

06

Save unexpected money

If you receive money you weren't expecting, consider putting some or all of it towards your savings goal.
